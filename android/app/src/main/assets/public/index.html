Why Choose Us

Everything You Need
for Smart Transit

A complete university bus management ecosystem designed for the modern campus.

Real-Time GPS Tracking
Watch buses move live on an interactive map. Updated every 3 seconds via WebSocket for pinpoint accuracy.
Accurate ETA Calculation
Smart ETA for every stop based on real-time position and speed. Never miss your bus again.
Instant Notifications
Real-time push alerts for delays, cancellations, and emergency announcements direct to your dashboard.
Seat Availability
Live seat occupancy indicators so you know if there's space before the bus arrives.
Full Route Mapping
View complete routes with all stops plotted on the map. Interactive polylines with stop tooltips.
Secure & Role-Based
JWT authentication, bcrypt encryption, and strict role-based access control for students, managers, and admins.
Analytics & Reports
Comprehensive admin reports on bus utilization, delays, route statistics, and student ridership trends.
QR Bus Identification
Each bus has a unique QR code for quick identification and attendance tracking on campus.
Dark Mode & Responsive
Beautiful dark mode and fully responsive design — works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops.

How It Works

One System, Four Unified Roles

Real-time coordination between university administration, fleet managers, bus drivers, and students.

01
ADMIN
Manages user accounts, route networks, geofences, and monitors audit logs.
02
MANAGER
Oversees assigned fleet units, controls trip status, and tracks bus health.
03
DRIVER
Logs in on mobile, starts assigned trip, and streams continuous real GPS.
04
STUDENT
Watches live bus motion, views real-time stop ETAs, and catches their ride on time.
